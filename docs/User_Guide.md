# User Guide: Allies Connect

Welcome to **Allies Connect**! This platform connects Georgia residents with community resources and volunteer opportunities.

This guide covers how to use the platform as a **Volunteer** or a **Service Provider**.

---

## 1. Accessing the Platform
Visit the homepage: [`https://alliesdemo.org/`](https://alliesdemo.org/).
- **Public View**: Anyone can browse opportunities and resources without an account.
- **Account Required**: You must create an account to *sign up* for volunteer shifts or to *post* new opportunities.

---

## 2. For Service Providers (Organizations)
 
 ### How to Register as an Organization
 1.  Click the **Register** button.
 2. On the **Create Account** page, enter the following information:
    - **Name**: Enter your **Name** (or your Organization's Representative name).
    - **Account Type**: **Important!** Select **"Service Provider (Organization)"**.
    - **Email**: Use your organization email.
    - **Password**: Must be at least 8 characters.
 3.  Click **Register**. You will be redirected to the **Provider Dashboard**.
 
 ### The Provider Dashboard
 Once logged in as a Provider, a **"Dashboard"** link will appear in the top navigation bar (next to your name). Click this link to manage your listings.
 
 From here, you can:
 -   **My Opportunities**: View and edit your active volunteer posts.
 -   **Create New**: Post a new opportunity.
 
 ### How to Create an Opportunity
 1.  From the [Dashboard](https://alliesdemo.org/provider/dashboard), click **Create Opportunity**.
 2.  Fill in the form:
     -   **Opportunity Title**: A clear headline (e.g., "Food Pantry Assistant").
     -   **Description**: Details about the role, location, and requirements.
     -   **Zip Code**: Enter the location zip code.
     -   **Date**: Select the date of the event.
 3.  **Volunteer Shifts**:
     -   Click **"+ Add Shift"**.
     -   Specify a **Start Time** and **End Time**.
     -   Set **Capacity** (how many volunteers you need).
     -   Repeat to add multiple shifts (e.g., Morning Shift, Afternoon Shift).
 4.  **Volunteer Questions** (Optional):
     -   Click **"+ Add Question"** to ask volunteers specific details (e.g., "T-shirt size", "Dietary restrictions").
     -   Enter the question text and select a type (Short Text, Long Text, Yes/No).
     -   Check **Required** if an answer is mandatory. (This does not apply for Yes/No questions, which generate a checkbox.)
 5.  Click **Create Opportunity**.
     -   *The opportunity is now live and visible to the public!*
 
 ---
 
 ## 3. For Volunteers (Public Users)
 
 ### How to Register
 1.  Click **Register** in the top navigation bar.
 2.  Fill in your details:
     -   **Name**: Your full name.
     -   **Account Type**: Select **"Volunteer (Public)"**.
     -   **Email**: Your personal email address.
     -   **Password**: Must be at least 8 characters.
 3.  Click **Register**. You will be automatically logged in and redirected to the home page.
 
 ### How to Sign Up for a Shift
 1.  **Browse Opportunities**: On the [**Volunteer Opportunities**](https://alliesdemo.org/opportunities) page, click on an opportunity card to view details.
 2.  **View Shifts**: Review the "Available Shifts" section.
 3.  **Sign Up**: Click the shift time that works best for you.
 4. If there are any questions, answer them.
 5. Click **Sign Up for Shift**.
 6.  **Confirm**: Your status will change to "Pending" or "Confirmed" depending on the organization's settings.

## Troubleshooting
-   **"Failed to fetch"**: If you see this error, please contact the site administrator. It usually means the server is down or there is a connection issue.
-   **Login Failed**: Double-check your email and password. Passwords are case-sensitive.
