# User Guide: Allies Connect

Welcome to **Allies Connect**! This platform connects Georgia residents with community resources and volunteer opportunities.

This guide covers how to use the platform as a **Volunteer** or a **Service Provider**.

---

## 1. Accessing the Platform
Visit the homepage (e.g., `http://<your-server-ip>/`).
- **Public View**: Anyone can browse opportunities and resources without an account.
- **Account Required**: You must create an account to *sign up* for volunteer shifts or to *post* new opportunities.

---

## 2. For Service Providers (Organizations)
 
 ### How to Register as an Organization
 1.  Go to the **Register** page.
 2.  **Name**: Enter your **Name** (or your Organization's Representative name).
 3.  **Account Type**: **Important!** Select **"Service Provider (Organization)"**.
 4.  **Email**: Use your organization email.
 5.  Click **Register**. You will be redirected to the **Provider Dashboard**.
 
 ### The Provider Dashboard
 Once logged in as a Provider, a **"Dashboard"** link will appear in the top navigation bar (next to your name). Click this link to manage your listings.
 
 From here, you can:
 -   **My Opportunities**: View and edit your active volunteer posts.
 -   **Create New**: Post a new opportunity.
 
 ### How to Create an Opportunity
 1.  From the Dashboard, click **"Create Opportunity"**.
 2.  Fill in the form:
     -   **Title**: A clear headline (e.g., "Food Pantry Assistant").
     -   **Description**: Details about the role, location, and requirements.
     -   **Organization**: Select your organization profile.
 3.  **Add Shifts**:
     -   Select **Start Time** and **End Time**.
     -   Set **Capacity** (how many volunteers you need).
     -   Click **"Add Shift"** to add multiple slots (e.g., Morning Shift, Afternoon Shift).
 4.  Click **Create Opportunity**.
     -   *The opportunity is now live and visible to the public!*
 
 ---
 
 ## 3. For Volunteers (Public Users)
 
 ### How to Register
 1.  Click **Sign In** in the top navigation bar.
 2.  Click the **Register** link next to the text **"Don't have an account?"**.
 3.  Fill in your details:
     -   **Name**: Your full name.
     -   **Account Type**: Select **"Volunteer (Public)"**.
     -   **Email**: Your personal email address.
     -   **Password**: Must be at least 8 characters.
 4.  Click **Register**. You will be automatically logged in and redirected to the home page.
 
 ### How to Sign Up for a Shift
 1.  **Browse**: On the homepage or "Opportunities" list, click on an opportunity card to view details.
 2.  **View Shifts**: Scroll down to the "Available Shifts" section.
 3.  **Sign Up**: Click the **Sign Up** button next to a shift time.
     -   *Note: If you are not logged in, you will be prompted to login/register first.*
 4.  **Confirm**: Your status will change to "Pending" or "Confirmed" depending on the organization's settings.

## 4. Managing Your Account
-   **Logout**: Click your profile icon/name in the top right to log out.
-   **Profile Settings**: (Coming Soon) Update your password or contact info.

## Troubleshooting
-   **"Failed to fetch"**: If you see this error, please contact the site administrator. It usually means the server is down or there is a connection issue.
-   **Login Failed**: Double-check your email and password. Passwords are case-sensitive.
